How to play

Rules

  1. Pick the winner of every game.
  2. The final deadline to submit your picks is the beginning of the early window Sunday games (12:00 pm CT / 10:00 am PT). Games before this time (TNF, International, etc.) must be picked before kickoff.
  3. Games that aren't picked before their deadline are auto-picked for the Away team.
  4. If multiple people tie for the week win, the tiebreaker is determined by a runoff to the following week, only between those people. Ties in week 18 or the season leaderboard are split.
